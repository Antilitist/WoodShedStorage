description=
description=[h1]FEATURES[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Custom 2x2 wood-shed sprites (chicken-coop footprint)
description=[*] Hit R: open bay south or east
description=[*] Building menu > Furniture > Wood Shed and Wood Shed (Lean-to)
description=[*] One storage bay. Click any side, same loot window
description=[*] Padlockable
description=[*] Sledge or Disassemble takes down the whole shed (no leftover wall, no hole). Disassemble gives some planks, nails, and logs back
description=[*] Sandbox option: capacity 50-2000 (default 400)
description=[*] Sandbox toggle: Use JB Max Capacity Override (default on)
description=[*] Capacity above 100 is optional (JB Max Capacity Override)
description=[*] Carpentry / Woodwork 4
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]OPTIONAL[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3452113500]JB Max Capacity Override[/url] (Workshop 3452113500) for caps above 100
description=[*] Starlit Library (required by JB)
description=[/list]
description=
description=Without JB, or with Use JB off, the bay is vanilla 100. Same Workshop item. No extra variant.
description=

description=[list]
description=[*] Hammer (kept)
description=[*] 10 Planks
description=[*] 10 Nails
description=[*] 6 Logs
